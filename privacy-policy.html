Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ftelia Yachting M.C.P.Y. ("we", "us", "NAIA") collects, uses, and protects the personal data you provide when you contact us through this website, www.naiayacht.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Preferred charter dates
  • Number of guests
  • Any message or additional details you choose to include

We do not collect payment information, identification documents, or any other personal data through this website.

How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to respond to your charter enquiry — to get in touch with you, discuss availability, and provide details about M/Y NAIA. We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond replying to your enquiry.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your enquiry is handled directly by our team and, where relevant to progressing a charter, may be shared with the yacht's central agent or brokerage acting on our behalf — never for advertising or resale purposes.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to you and, if a charter proceeds, for the duration of the booking process. You may request deletion of your data at any time as set out below.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict how your data is processed

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
