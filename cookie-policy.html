Cookie Policy

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies this website uses and why. We keep our use of cookies to a minimum, in line with our approach to your privacy.

What Cookies We Use

This website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function correctly, and a small number of resources from Google Fonts used to load our typefaces (Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat). These font files are served from Google's servers and may involve a minimal, non-identifying technical request to load correctly.

We also use your browser's local storage to remember that you have dismissed the cookie banner on this site, so it does not reappear on every visit.

No Tracking or Advertising Cookies

We do not use analytics, tracking, retargeting, or advertising cookies of any kind. We do not build a profile of your browsing activity, and no data collected here is used for advertising purposes, on this site or elsewhere.

Managing Cookies

Because this site uses only essential cookies, there is nothing to opt out of that would affect core functionality. If you would prefer not to load fonts from Google's servers, or wish to clear stored preferences, you can do so through your browser's settings — typically found under Privacy & Security, or Site Settings. Disabling essential cookies or local storage may affect how parts of this website display or function.
